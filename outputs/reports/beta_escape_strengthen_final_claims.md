# Defensible Claims

- Mean high-beta power conflates amplitude, occupancy, and persistence (measurement non-identifiability).
- Beta-state persistence metrics are mathematically separable from mean power.
- SMR neurofeedback learning is subject to extreme inter-individual dynamical variability.

*Excluded claims*: We cannot claim that beta escape (persistence change) is the primary driver of high-beta suppression, nor can we claim it strongly or universally predicts SMR acquisition in this dataset.
