# Audit 4: Threshold-Free State Validation

1. **What features were used in the GMM?** Log power of SMR, High-Beta, and Broadband envelopes.
2. **Was the number of states fixed or selected?** Fixed at 2 components (high beta vs low beta state).
3. **Was the high-beta state identified without using the threshold labels?** Yes, by selecting the component with the highest mean log beta power.
4. **Is Dice > 0.6 compared against chance?** No. The mean Dice coefficient was ~0.449, which is very weak agreement.
5. **Is agreement consistent across subjects and sessions?** It is consistently weak across all subjects.
6. **Does the GMM state reproduce dwell/transition findings?** Since the GMM states disagree with the threshold states >50% of the time, they are defining fundamentally different dynamical regimes.
