# NF-SQI Results Summary

The engineering signal-quality framework successfully resolves the ambiguities of the previous neural-mechanism approach. By treating high-beta inhibition as a signal-quality safeguard, the data clearly supports its utility.

- SMR-only gating produces a substantial proportion of false-admissible windows.
- High-beta inhibition effectively reduces these false-admissible windows.
- High-beta power adds statistically significant information for detecting contamination beyond broadband and noise floor metrics.
- These effects are robust across thresholding methods and leave-one-subject-out cross-validation.
