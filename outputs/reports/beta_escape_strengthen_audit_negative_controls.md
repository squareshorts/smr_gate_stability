# Audit 3: Negative-Control Band Validity

1. **Are thresholds comparable across bands?** Yes, p75 was used uniformly.
2. **Does percentile thresholding artificially equalize occupancy across bands?** Yes, forcing the threshold at the 75th percentile normalizes the baseline occupancy to ~25% for all bands, heavily constraining variance.
3. **Is high-beta specificity present?** The mean dwell time change for high beta was 0.0004s (p=0.79). The effect is completely absent at the group level across all bands.
4. **Does the 35–45 Hz control rule out broadband/EMG?** Since neither high beta nor the control showed significant group-level persistence changes, we cannot conclusively rule out anything.
5. **Does low beta show similar effects?** Low beta also showed non-significant changes (p=0.16).
6. **Is the effect truly high-beta-specific, beta-family-specific, or generic?** The effect is statistically non-existent at the group level when parameterized this way, implying extreme individual variability or a generic null result.
