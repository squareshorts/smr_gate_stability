# NF-SQI Claims

## Supported Claims
- **High-beta inhibition is a signal-quality safeguard**: It reduces false-admissible SMR feedback states.
- **SMR-only rules are vulnerable**: They admit heterogeneous, contaminated windows.
- **High-beta adds distinct information**: It captures artifacts or broadband shifts not fully captured by high-frequency or broadband noise metrics alone.

## Unsupported Claims
- **Neural mechanism claims**: We do not claim high beta represents an active damping neural mechanism.
- **State persistence necessity**: We do not claim beta-state persistence is required for SMR learning.
