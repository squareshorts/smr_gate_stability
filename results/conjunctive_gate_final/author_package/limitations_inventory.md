# Limitations inventory

- No natural-artifact ground truth; the reproducibility target is between-replicate accepted-set agreement.
- Composition prediction is exact only under independence; criteria are positively dependent, so the
  independence prediction is a conservative approximation.
- R1 does not eliminate all temporal switching (matched-availability transition reduction ~15%, below 30%).
- Controlled degradation is engineering measurement-degradation testing, not natural-artifact validation.
- R1 has degradation blind spots: clipping and channel-freeze (top response ~0.0), and reduced
  sensitivity to single-channel broadband (0.54) and 20-30 Hz (0.58); its stability is partly due to
  insensitivity to localized single-channel faults.
- Three-channel sensorimotor montage (E36,E104,E128); three datasets; retrospective replay.
- No prospective neurofeedback-efficacy test.
- R2 is sensitivity only; R3 is a negative comparator.
- No regulatory or clinical-safety claim.
