# Stage 1 frozen success criteria

A calibration method passes only if: pooled median accepted-set Jaccard >= 0.85; at least 80% of sessions have accepted-set Jaccard >= 0.80; pooled median overall decision agreement >= 0.97; no dataset has median accepted-set Jaccard < 0.75; and no dataset loses more than 25% of sessions because calibration is nonviable. The stronger target Jaccard 0.90 is descriptive, not the minimum GO criterion.
